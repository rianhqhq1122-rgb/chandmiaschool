Curriculum Overview

Chand Mia High School follows the national curriculum prescribed by the Ministry of Education, Bangladesh. Students are prepared for the Secondary School Certificate (SSC) examination conducted by the Board of Intermediate and Secondary Education.

Grades 6-8

Foundation subjects including Bangla, English, Mathematics, Science, Social Science, and Religious Studies.

Grades 9-10 (SSC)

Specialized curriculum with Science, Humanities, and Business Studies groups. Preparation for SSC board examination.

Co-curricular

ICT education, physical education, arts and crafts, and various club activities for holistic development.
